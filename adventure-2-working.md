# Adventure 2 working — Cursed Crypts of Ambergul

Opened 2026-10-11. Party level 4, XP 2890. Module outranks this file. Table outranks the module. Do not quote the PDF. Adventures 3–4 stay closed.

## Plot lines still in play

- Red Wizards of Thay want four elemental keys. The Earth Key (a prickly ball) was hidden in the altar of Ogrémoch under the old Ambergul estate. Darwa Dalion (“Boo Boo Doll”), Arvik’s apprentice, led a small expedition there. She was turned to stone by a trap after the altar was smashed. Thord (dwarf bodyguard) was taken by minotaurs. Grit (his dworc son) and Lump (ogrillon) got out with the key.
- 1,000 gp bounty, alive, on Darwa. Fifty for information. Last seen with Arvik, red robes and black wig. Accused of conspiracy and theft from Lord Floshin. Real Maechen Givens (missing left ear) is the one nailing the poster; she has never met the party.
- Onya Olchabar, pregnant, is looking for her lover Arassán. He fell in with Darwa’s crew and spoke of treasure under a ruined estate near Cromm’s Hold. She wants the party, as friends of Isteval, to find him without the duke’s guards arresting him.
- Nadir the rakshasa (the face from Adventure 1) is not in this dungeon. It has taken Arvik’s face and is heading for the dungeons under Mount Illefarn to hunt the Fire Key. Time pressure is soft: Isteval or someone else may eventually notice the shape is still moving.
- Adult black dragon is still alive under the lake. Wyrmling went to it. Blue coral altar (Olhydra) was seen and not touched. Those threads are live but not the job of this adventure.

## Places

- Daggerford: tavern (opening scene), market square (puppet show of the “dragon slayers”), Delfen’s Tower. Horses still to be returned.
- Ambergul estate ruins, south of Daggerford off the Trade Way, near Cromm’s Hold. Mansion gone. Family crypts aboveground (waterlogged ground, boot prints everywhere). Secret entrance from the crypts into the dungeon complex.
- Dungeon complex under the estate: galleries, halls, vault, altar of Ogrémoch (area 28). Ceilings 10 ft. Some rooms already looted or broken by Darwa’s group.

- Darwa Dalion: petrified near the altar area after the trap. Can be restored or left.
- Thord: dwarf, alive, held by minotaurs deeper in. Knows the key is gone.
- Grit and Lump: left with the Earth Key. Not present.
- Minotaurs: in the lower dungeon. One or more have Thord.
- Undead Amberguls: ghosts and other remains in the true crypts (areas 9a–9d and related). Curses on some tombs. Olivine, Kamal, Alicia, Gaston, Reynard.
- Animated armor and other guardians Reynard left.

- Maechen Givens and Brannis Coldriver: town watch, poster scene only.
- Onya Olchabar and her father Mylo: outside town. She is the soft hook into the ruins.
